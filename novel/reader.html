Chapter 1

中文1

灯光从天花板落下时，房间的每一寸空气都像被重新审讯了一遍。她站在镜子前，低头看着自己的影子，仿佛正在等待一项从未真正完成的结论。

评估开始于规矩，而不是情绪。审视的目光比任何语言都更准确，像一只无形的手在皮肤上敲打，试图从每一处细节里挑出非自然的面孔。

她不急着解释，也不急着辩白。因为答案早就在那一瞬间被写进了玻璃和空气里，只等被人看见。

English1

When the light fell from the ceiling, every inch of the room seemed to be interrogated all over again. She stood before the mirror, lowering her gaze to her own shadow, as if awaiting a verdict that had never truly been completed.

The assessment began with rules, not emotion. The inspecting gaze was more precise than any language, like an invisible hand tapping on the skin, trying to pull out the unnatural from each subtle detail.

She did not rush to explain, nor did she rush to defend herself. Because the answer had already been written into the glass and the air at that very moment, waiting only for someone to notice it.

Chapter 2

中文2

沉默在房间里慢慢成形，像一块石头被磨成更多的轮廓。她终于抬起头，眼神不再躲闪，反而带着一点疲惫后的清醒。

当那条边界被轻轻划开时，真正的分界并不在于谁先退一步，而在于谁终于承认：被看见，未必意味着被理解。

故事并没有真的结束，它只是把呼吸从表层移到了更深处，像暮色落在木纹上，轮廓愈发清晰。

English2

Silence gradually took shape in the room, like a stone being polished into finer contours. At last, she raised her head, her gaze no longer evasive, but carrying the clarity that follows exhaustion.

When that boundary was gently opened, the real divide was not who stepped back first, but who finally admitted that being seen did not necessarily mean being understood.

The story had not truly ended; it had simply moved breathing from the surface to a deeper place, like dusk falling across wood grain, making the outlines more distinct.

tomodachi · Chapter 1

中文1

清晨的门口没有人说话，只有风从走廊里绕过，带着一点湿润与微弱的晨光。她站在门边，像一位还没有决定是否需要去敲门的人。

他们曾经以为熟悉能解决一切，直到某个不经意的停顿，让彼此都意识到：真正的在场，往往来自沉默之后的理解。

English1

No one spoke at the morning doorway; only the wind passed through the corridor, carrying a hint of moisture and a strand of pale sunlight. She stood at the door, like someone who had not yet decided whether to knock.

They once believed familiarity could solve everything, until one accidental pause made both of them realize that true presence often comes from understanding that follows silence.

tomodachi · Chapter 2

中文2

音乐从窗边淡淡流进来，像一段没有歌词的旧信。她的手指停在键盘上，久久没有落下，仿佛想把话语留在空气里，再等一等。

某种温柔不是轰然出现，而是在试探中逐渐成形；它像灯光一点点亮起，又在不被看见时悄悄落回去。

English2

The music drifted in from the window like an old letter without words. Her fingers hovered over the keys, not moving for a long time, as if she wanted to leave the words suspended in the air a little longer.

A certain gentleness does not appear in a grand blaze, but gradually takes shape through trial and error; it glows like a light turning on, then quietly settles back into darkness when unseen.

school · Chapter 1

中文1

放学后的走廊比平时更安静，仿佛每一扇窗都在悄悄听别人呼吸。她把书包放在桌角，像在一场不会被记录的对话前，先给自己留出一点余地。

校园从来不缺暗示，它只需要一个看似偶然的动作，和一个不肯转开的目光。

English1

The corridor after school was quieter than usual, as if every window were listening quietly to someone breathing. She set her bag in the corner of the desk, as if leaving a little room for herself before a conversation that would never be recorded.

School never lacked hints; it only needed one seemingly accidental gesture and one gaze that refused to turn away.

school · Chapter 2

中文2

她把名字写在纸边，字迹却像被风吹过的云，既真的又不真实。那一刻，所有关于身份、年龄与答案的解释，都像从门缝里漏出来的光。

他们都知道，青春最容易被误解，因为它总会把温柔装成一种迟疑，把勇敢说成一种无法自持的沉默。

English2

She wrote her name on the edge of the page, yet the handwriting felt like clouds blown by wind—both real and unreal. At that moment, all explanations about identity, age, and answers seemed to leak out like light through a crack in the door.

They all knew that youth was easily misunderstood because it always dressed gentleness as hesitation and courage as a silence one could not sustain.
