Chapter 1

English1

The corridor after school was quieter than usual, as if every window were listening quietly to someone breathing. She set her bag in the corner of the desk, as if leaving a little room for herself before a conversation that would never be recorded.

School never lacked hints; it only needed one seemingly accidental gesture and one gaze that refused to turn away.

中文1

放学后的走廊比平时更安静，仿佛每一扇窗都在悄悄听别人呼吸。她把书包放在桌角，像在一场不会被记录的对话前，先给自己留出一点余地。

校园从来不缺暗示，它只需要一个看似偶然的动作，和一个不肯转开的目光。

Chapter 2

English2

She wrote her name on the edge of the page, yet the handwriting felt like clouds blown by wind—both real and unreal. At that moment, all explanations about identity, age, and answers seemed to leak out like light through a crack in the door.

They all knew that youth was easily misunderstood because it always dressed gentleness as hesitation and courage as a silence one could not sustain.

中文2

他们都知道，青春最容易被误解，因为它总会把温柔装成一种迟疑，把勇敢说成一种无法自持的沉默。
