You waited until everyone in the locker room had left before leaving the swimming pool. Whitney threw your clothes in the women's locker room, and you were worried that the girls would see your body. Only now did you dare to retrieve your clothes. Fortunately, there were no students left in either the men's or women's locker rooms, and you successfully retrieved your clothes. However, when you finished changing and tried to leave, Whitney blocked your way at the door. He was surrounded by several other students, all taller and stronger than you. They had already entered puberty and looked like men, while you were still just a thin boy, only reaching their shoulders. You had a thin chest and no Adam's apple, and the students looked at you with mockery and contempt. One of the students said, "Hey, did anyone see that shorty Tix just went into the girls' locker room? Is she a girl? Hahaha." One student chimed in, "Yeah, haha, only girls go to the women's locker room. Since you're a girl, what are you doing in the men's locker room?" "Are you here to steal something? Or to secretly leave love letters? Ugh... you're disgusting, no one will like you!" "Let's take a look at his schoolbag; there must be a cheesy love letter inside!" One of them snatched your backpack. You tried to get it back, but he passed it to another student like a ball. You ran to another student, but before you could get there, your backpack was in the hands of a third person. You were being kicked around like a ball by the group of students. Finally, the backpack came to a stop in Whitney's hands. He held it high, stood still, and looked down at you. "Give me back my backpack." You tiptoe to grab your backpack, but Whitney is just too tall. Once he raises his hand, you have no chance at all. You jump up and try to pull his sleeve, but all your efforts are like a teacup dog trying to fight off a gray wolf. In other people's eyes, it's just a joke. I don't know who kicked you. "Ugh..." You collapsed to the ground in pain. "How dare you get Whitney's clothes dirty! Do you know what you're going to do?" Whitney's students shouted. You lie on the ground, and when you look up, you see the boys' shoes and calves. They have dark, healthy skin and masculine leg hair. They are as strong and muscular as rugby players. The floor of the men's locker room was still damp. You didn't try to get up; instead, you instinctively curled up your small body. You knew you were about to get beaten up, and getting up or doing anything to resist would only make them beat you harder. It was better to curl up and protect yourself, at least to avoid getting a black eye and swollen face like before... You tell yourself not to struggle, not to cry for help, and try not to cry. Just remain unresponsive, like a puppet, and wait for them to hit you. They'll get bored and leave... However, the expected beating did not come. You secretly opened your eyes and saw that the boys' bodies had turned in a different direction, and the toes of their shoes were pointing in the same spot. Looking past their calves, you see a pair of cute women's shoes. Following the slender yet strong calves upwards, you see a black school uniform skirt that falls below the knee. The girl is rather thin; even with the belt pulled tightest, the skirt doesn't hug her body, looking loose and baggy. You don't know why she chose such an oversized skirt that clearly doesn't fit her, but the loose clothing only amplifies her cold and aloof demeanor. Looking further up, you see a girl with long, straight, thick black hair that reaches her chest. She deliberately pulls some hair forward to cover her shoulders and chest, and she has bangs long enough to cover her eyebrows. Judging from her appearance, she is a very shy girl who always tries to hide herself. Right now, she's standing in the corridor between the men's and women's locker rooms, holding a few books, looking at you. Somewhat unexpectedly, there's no fear or embarrassment in her expression; even facing the most powerful bullying group on campus, she doesn't resist making eye contact. You can't even see any emotion in her deep black eyes; her eyes are like the surface of a frozen lake, cold, indifferent, and without any ripples. You know her; she's a third-grade student, almost two years older than you. You once had the same math class with her, and her aloof and seemingly unapproachable demeanor left a deep impression on you. The boys started whispering among themselves, and surprisingly, they all knew the girl. You had no idea what they were talking about or what they planned to do to her. Lying on the ground, you were only thinking about protecting yourself, but for some reason, you suddenly wanted to loudly warn the girl to leave immediately. If you had the chance, you would even be willing to hug Whitney's leg and use your body to buy time for the girl so that she could avoid being bullied like you. However, before you could do anything, the girl left on her own. She went into the women's changing room and came out a short while later, wearing a white headset. She kept her head down, focused on her phone, and didn't look at you all again as she left, as if you didn't exist at all, as if to her you were just a pile of roadside trash that she had only glanced at once and then lost all interest in. The girl's appearance interrupted Whitney and his friends' bullying of you. After the girl left, the boys seemed to have reached a conclusion; they lost interest in continuing to beat you up, just like robbers who, upon discovering the police were nearby, could only leave dejectedly. Besides Whitney, he was also somewhat resentful: "She's just a skinny girl, and she doesn't even have any companions..." Another boy advised him, "It's best not to mess with her. I've heard that people who mess with her never end up well..." The boys threw their backpacks next to you and left without giving you another glance. You remained lying on the ground, and after a long while, you still couldn't understand your current situation. You weren't beaten up, and you didn't get the chance to be a hero and save the damsel in distress as you might have imagined. It seems that the girl's appearance saved you, and Whitney and his companions walked away... You get up from the damp floor, grab your backpack, and something's still a jumble—why would a bully like Whitney know that girl? Why are they afraid of her? And what's her name...? You've only taken a few classes with her, the girl doesn't talk much, you live far apart, and you have a bad memory... But after some thought, you finally remember her name—it's Deco.
